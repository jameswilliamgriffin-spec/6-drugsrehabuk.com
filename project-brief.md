# Project Brief — drugsrehabuk.com (Site 6)

## Network Context
This site is part of a seven-site microsite network 
supporting The Wellbourne Clinic. It is the first site 
in the network focused on drug addiction rather than alcohol.

Hub site: https://thewellbourneclinic.co.uk/

## This Site's Purpose
Drug-specific authority site. Educational and clinical, 
covering multiple substance categories rather than one.

Theme: drug rehab and detox authority — the equivalent 
role to alcoholdetoxrehab.com, but for drugs.

## Tone
- Clear, calm, clinical — closer to site 1 than site 5
- Educational rather than editorial
- Avoid sensationalising drug use or withdrawal
- Avoid stigmatising language — addiction is a health condition, 
  not a moral failing
- Think: NHS drug information pages, Verywell Mind, Mayo Clinic

## Stack
- Next.js (App Router)
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion for scroll animations and interactions
- Match the project structure and conventions used in sites 1-5

## Brand Guidelines
- Primary brand colour: #F19885
- Wellbourne Clinic logo in header and footer
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans (display) + DM Sans (body)
- Same colour palette as established for the network

## Content Clusters
Unlike sites 1-5, this site organises around substance type, 
not a single linear journey. Build a hub-and-spoke structure:

### Pillar Page
Drug Rehab — overview, what drug rehab involves, who it's for

### Substance Clusters
**Cocaine**
- Cocaine addiction and withdrawal
- Cocaine detox
- Crack cocaine addiction

**Cannabis**
- Cannabis and mental health
- Cannabis dependency
- Cannabis withdrawal

**Prescription Drugs**
- Diazepam (benzodiazepine) addiction
- Codeine addiction
- Tramadol dependency

**Heroin / Opioids**
- Heroin addiction and risks
- Opioid withdrawal and detox

## Primary Keywords
- drug rehab uk
- drug detox
- drug addiction treatment
- cocaine addiction
- cocaine rehab
- prescription drug addiction
- heroin addiction treatment
- drug withdrawal symptoms

## Page Structure
1. Hero — clear, authoritative, drug-focused
2. What Is Drug Rehab? (overview)
3. Common Substances We Treat (cards linking to substance clusters)
4. The Drug Detox Process
5. Withdrawal Symptoms by Substance Category
6. Drug Rehab vs Drug Detox (comparison table)
7. Why Choose Professional Treatment
8. FAQ Section (schema markup)
9. Substance-Specific Guides (premium cards)
10. Final CTA
